Our Story

A Heritage Since 1895

Baltimore Coffee & Tea has been roasting and blending exceptional coffees and teas for over a century. What began as a small local trade has grown into one of the Mid-Atlantic’s most trusted specialty roasters.

We source directly from growers we know personally, roast in small batches to peak perfection, and ship fresh to your door — often the same day your order comes in.
